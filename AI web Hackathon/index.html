About Edupath

Guiding students from confusion to clarity.

Edupath was created to solve a challenge many students face: choosing a future without clear direction. From Class 10 to graduation, students are often overwhelmed by endless options, uncertain goals and limited guidance.

We simplify educational decisions through career assessment, mentor support, college discovery, skill-building insights and personalized roadmaps. Mentorship matters because the right guidance can transform a student's confidence, choices and outcome.

Frequently asked questions

Answers to help you get started.

Who is Edupath for?

Edupath is designed for students from Class 10 to undergraduate graduates who need guidance on careers, colleges and mentors.

Does Edupath provide career match suggestions?

Yes. The platform uses a transparent, rule-based recommendation engine based on interests, strengths and academic goals.

Can I connect with mentors?

Absolutely. Students can explore mentor profiles, review expertise and book mentorship sessions.

Can I compare colleges?

Yes. Students can browse colleges, filter by stream and compare shortlisted options side by side.
